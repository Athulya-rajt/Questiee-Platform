Platform Aim

Questiee transforms complex tech concepts—like network routing, ethical hacking, and data manipulation—into digestible quest levels. Master concepts through repetition, hands-on code writing, and instant feedback.

Gameplay Rules & Instructions
3 Lives Per Quest Incorrect answers deplete lives. Protect your health bar to finish all 30 questions!
1 Skip Life Stuck on a hard question? Use your single skip lifeline strategically per quest.
3 Escalating Levels Progress through Level 1 (Trivia), Level 2 (Core Concepts), to Level 3 (Python Coding).
Hint System Every question includes tailored hints to guide your solution without giving it away immediately.
